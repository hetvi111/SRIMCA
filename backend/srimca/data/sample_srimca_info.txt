SRIMCA full name is Shrimad Rajchandra Institute of Management and Computer Application.

SRIMCA is located at Maliba Campus, Gopal Vidyanagar, Bardoli-Mahuva Road, Tarsadi, Surat, Gujarat 394350.

SRIMCA is a constituent institute of Uka Tarsadia University (UTU).

SRIMCA offers Master of Computer Applications (MCA), Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), Integrated MCA (5-year), Master of Business Administration (MBA), and Integrated MBA (IMBA).

All programmes at SRIMCA are approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and Government of Gujarat.

MCA programme at SRIMCA was established in the year 2002.

MBA programme at SRIMCA was established in the year 2004.

SRIMCA vision is to become a globally recognized premier educational institute for academic excellence, innovation, and character building.

SRIMCA mission is to remain on the cutting edge of education, research, industry partnerships, and social commitment.

SRIMCA provides state-of-the-art computer labs with more than 250 high-end computers and modern software.

SRIMCA campus is equipped with a 200 Mbps dedicated high-speed internet leased line and campus-wide Wi-Fi.

SRIMCA library has an extensive collection of national and international books, IEEE, ACM journals, and digital e-resources.

SRIMCA facilities include air-conditioned auditorium, modern seminar halls, smart interactive classrooms, cafeteria, and sports complex.

SRIMCA provides hostel facilities for boys and girls with 24/7 security and Wi-Fi.

SRIMCA provides bus transportation facilities covering Surat, Navsari, Bardoli, Vyara, and surrounding areas.

Admission in MCA is conducted via ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses) and Management Quota.

Admission in BCA is conducted directly through Uka Tarsadia University (UTU) admission portal.
